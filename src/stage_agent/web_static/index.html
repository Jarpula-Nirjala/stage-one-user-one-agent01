How it works

The decision loop

1 · Understand

Facts carry source, time and freshness. Conflicts between profile, app setting and campaign link are resolved by explicit precedence and kept visible.

2 · Choose a goal

One measurable goal from the catalogue, with a deadline and a success signal, or an explained abstention. Unmeasurable goals are never chosen.

3 · Decide and act

A personalised home page, one proportionate message, or nothing, inside a 60 second and 8 model / 20 tool call budget.

4 · Verify

A separate verifier checks eligibility, dialect, availability, consent, quiet hours, frequency, copy and holdout, and can reject any action.

5 · Close the loop

Goals are judged from attributable events and the lessons are written back into each viewer's state.

6 · Prove impact

Intention-to-treat on randomized arms with confidence intervals. The biased served-versus-rest comparison is shown only for contrast.
All figures come from replaying the supplied synthetic data pack. The raw pack is personal data and is not hosted here: this site serves only the run's outputs and a synthetic demonstration world.
